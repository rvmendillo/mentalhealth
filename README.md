# Renewed Mind

A Christ-centered mental-wellness app for iPhone and iPad. It combines Scripture-based affirmations, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) thought reframing, and evidence-based practices for the whole person — spiritual, emotional, mental, physical, social, and purpose.

> “Be transformed by the renewing of your mind.” — Romans 12:2

## What it does

- **Renew a thought.** Write down a painful or negative thought. Renewed Mind validates the feeling, names the thinking patterns involved (12 common CBT distortions), asks Socratic questions, and offers a balanced, truthful, hopeful perspective with a verified verse and word study.
- **Christ-based affirmations with healthy etymology.** 89 first-person affirmations across 15 themes. Each is anchored in an exact Berean Standard Bible verse and includes a Hebrew or Greek word study (Strong's number, lemma, meaning, insight). 25 English word-history cards (health, whole, and holy; comfort; anxiety; salvation; and more) — told carefully, without popular myths.
- **Evidence-based practices.** 20 practices — breath prayer (~6 breaths/min), cyclic sighing, Three Good Things, gratitude letters, acts of kindness, savoring, best possible self, self-compassion, movement, time in nature, reaching out, sleep wind-down, lament writing, Scripture meditation, the Examen, behavioral activation, strengths, forgiveness (REACH), Sabbath rest, and grounding. Each lists its research with DOIs.
- **A personalized "happiness algorithm."** Recommendations blend what you report today (check-ins), what has helped you before (Bayesian Thompson sampling over your "did this help?" ratings, with priors set by strength of evidence), variety to prevent hedonic adaptation, balance across wellness dimensions, and time of day.
- **Insights.** Mood trends, a weekly flourishing profile, the WHO-5 Well-Being Index, and what helps you most.
- **Safety first.** An on-device crisis screen runs before any AI. Crisis language is never sent to an AI model; the app leads with crisis lines and emergency numbers for your region (US 988, UK/IE Samaritans, AU Lifeline, NZ 1737, Canada 988, Philippines NCMH, India Tele-MANAS, and findahelpline.com elsewhere).

## AI models

The reflection guide works in three tiers, chosen automatically or in Settings:

1. **Claude (Anthropic)** — add your own API key in Settings (stored in the iOS Keychain). Defaults to **Claude Fable 5.2**, Anthropic's most capable model, with automatic fallback to Claude Opus 5.5 if your account can't use Fable. Also selectable: Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5. Requests use structured outputs; the model chooses a verse only from the app's verified catalog, so Scripture text is never generated by AI.
2. **Apple Intelligence (on-device)** — on iOS 26+ devices with Apple Intelligence, reflections are generated privately on the device.
3. **Offline guide** — a deterministic, fully offline CBT engine that always works.

## Install the IPA

The IPA from CI is **unsigned**. To install it on your iPhone, re-sign it with your Apple ID using a sideloading tool such as [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) or [AltStore](https://altstore.io), or sign it with your Apple Developer account. Requires iOS 17 or later; Apple Intelligence features require iOS 26 and a supported device.

## Build from source

Requirements: macOS with Xcode 26.

```sh
open RenewedMind.xcodeproj
```

The Xcode project is generated from `project.yml` with [XcodeGen](https://github.com/yonaskolb/XcodeGen). After changing `project.yml`, regenerate it with `brew install xcodegen && xcodegen generate`.

## Tests

- `Packages/RenewedMindKit`: 100+ unit tests for the crisis screen, distortion detection, reframing, recommendations, WHO-5 scoring, storage, the Claude client, and content integrity (`swift test`).
- `RenewedMindUITests`: end-to-end UI tests that drive the real app and capture screenshots.
- `Tools/validate_content.py`: checks every verse word-for-word against the BSB text and every Hebrew/Greek word against Strong's dictionary and the BSB interlinear.
- CI (`.github/workflows/ios.yml`) runs all of the above on iOS 26 and iOS 18 simulators and packages the unsigned IPA.

## Sources

- Scripture: Berean Standard Bible (BSB), dedicated to the public domain.
- Word studies: Strong's Exhaustive Concordance (public domain), checked against the Berean interlinear tables.
- Research citations: see the in-app "The science" page.

Renewed Mind is for encouragement and growth. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you are in crisis, call your local emergency number (in the US, call or text 988).
